Meet Coach Lu

25+ years of experience. A passion for teaching.

Coach Lu grew up in Washington and learned from renowned coach Jay Benner. She went on to swim at Whitworth University as an All-American and place second in Washington in the 100 Butterfly.

She now serves as a swim coach and teacher for the West Linn/Wilsonville and Lake Oswego school districts. Her philosophy centers on encouragement, confidence, water safety, and helping students believe they can become swimmers.

Each instructor is hand-selected and trained in water safety and the foundations of child development.
